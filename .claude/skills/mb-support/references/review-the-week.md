# Weekly review

Read `state/support/cases.md` and write one entry under `## Week of <date>` in
`state/support/review.md`, then send the owner the summary.

## The entry, in this order

1. **The check:** time of the last `Check:` line. If it is older than the flag
   interval, the first line says "No check ran in N hours" and why, if known.
   Missing checks matter more than anything else on the list.
2. **Open promises past due:** every row with a Due date before today and no
   Proof. Case id, what was promised, days late, what blocks it, the next step.
3. **Reopened cases:** each bounce, blank send or failed refund, with the next step.
4. **Waiting on others:** customer, supplier, owner. Name who and since when.
5. **Closed this week:** a count, and any whose Proof is thin.
6. **Repeats:** questions asked three times or more. Each is a candidate for a
   help article or a policy line. Propose it; do not write it unasked.
7. **Money:** every spend or refund this week, with the rule that allowed it. Flag
   anything that did not match `money-rules.md`.
8. **Proposal (optional):** at most one change to policy or money rules, with the
   cases that show why.

## The summary to the owner

Five lines or fewer: whether the check ran, the overdue promises (count and the
worst), anything waiting on the owner, the top repeat question, the one proposal
if any. Overdue promises are first. Do not ask what to do next week.
