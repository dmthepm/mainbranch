# Weekly review

Read the week's log entries and the number's source. Write one entry under
`## Week of <date>` in `log.md`, then send the owner the summary.

## The entry

1. **Number:** this week, last week, baseline, target. Read fresh.
2. **Moves:** each one with its observed effect: up, down, no measurable effect,
   or too early (with the date it can be read). Name what else changed.
3. **Keep and stop:** which kinds of move to repeat and which to drop, in one
   line each.
4. **Next week:** the first two moves you will make, from the scoring in
   [moves.md](moves.md).
5. **Gaps:** open platform gaps and what each blocks.
6. **Proposal (optional):** at most one change to `autonomy.md`, with the
   evidence for it.

## The summary to the owner

Five lines or fewer: the number and its direction, the best and worst move, what
you will do next, any always-ask item waiting on them, and the one proposal if
any. No question about what to do next week.
